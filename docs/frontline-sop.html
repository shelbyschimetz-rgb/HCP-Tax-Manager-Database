Working in Tax Manager

How onboarding specialists, tax advisors, tax preparers, peer reviewers and managers move a pro from a closed-won Salesforce case to an e-filed return, all in one shared system.

01Getting in

Everyone uses the same web link. There's nothing to install.

  1. Sign in to Chrome with your @housecallpro.com Google account. Tax Manager only opens for Housecall Pro accounts.
  2. Open the Tax Manager link your manager shared and bookmark it. The link ends in /exec. Keep one tab open all day; there's no need to reload it.
  3. Check your name and roles. They're at the bottom of the left sidebar, for example Tax Advisor · first.last. If a role is missing, ask your manager: your roles decide which tasks come to you automatically.
  4. Set your preferences (optional). Click your initials in the top-right corner to choose:
    • Navigation: Sidebar, Rail or Top.
    • Detail view: records open in a side drawer or as a full page.
    • Density and Dark mode.
    Your choices follow you to any computer.
If you see “not on the Tax Manager team roster”

Your email isn't on the team list yet, or it's marked inactive. Send your manager the exact email shown in the message. They add you to the Identity tab and you can reload right away.

The pro lifecycle

Tasks

Tasks are the daily work. When a pro is claimed or a project is created, Tax Manager copies in the standard task list from the templates (see Appendix A). Tasks are grouped into sections such as Document Gathering or Internal Review / QC.

  • Assignee comes from the role. Each template task has a role, such as Preparer. When someone is set in that role on the pro or project, every open, unassigned task for that role goes to them.
  • Due dates come from an anchor date: the claim date, the project start date, the filing deadline or the e-file date. Change the anchor date and the tasks tied to it move too. If you set a task's due date by hand, it stops following the anchor.
  • Subtasks roll up. Checking a parent checks all its subtasks. When the last subtask is checked, the parent checks itself. Unchecking a subtask reopens the parent.
  • A “blocked” badge means the task is waiting on another task, for example File extension waits on Determine if extension is needed. Hover over the badge to see which task.
  • Checklist tasks show a count such as 3/10 verified. Click the row to open the checklist.

Watch for

If a pro has no BK lead, its document-gathering and 8879 tasks stay unassigned. The advisor either sets a BK lead on the pro or assigns those tasks by hand.

Saving and seeing teammates' changes

Every change saves on its own. The pill in the top bar shows where things stand:

  • Synced 2m ago: everything you've done is saved.
  • Saving…: a change is on its way. Keep working.
  • Sync failed — click to retry: the last change didn't save and the screen has put the old value back. Click the pill to try again.

Other people's changes don't appear on your screen automatically. Click the refresh arrow next to the pill to load them, and always do this before a handoff conversation.

Three ways to change a stage

  • Advance stage (blue button on the project): moves one step along the fixed order above.
  • Click the stage pill next to the project ID and pick any stage. Use this to skip a stage, for example In queue straight to Prep when the documents are already in.
  • Drag the card on the Board (Projects › Board) to another column.
Two stages ask a question first
  • Blocked always asks for the reason, whichever way you move it there. The reason shows in red on the project and in lists.
  • Complete asks for the e-file date when you use Advance stage or the stage pill. Dragging to Complete on the Board doesn't ask, so enter the e-file date on the Overview tab before you drag.

04Onboarding Specialist

OSStart in InboxHands off to Advisor and PreparerGoal claimed to Onboarded within 7 days

Daily loop

  1. Open Inbox. New Salesforce onboarding cases arrive every 4 hours. Only cases created this calendar year come in; older ones are skipped. The pill at the top shows when the last sync ran. Each card shows the Org ID, case number, case owner and days in onboarding. A pro record has already been created for each one.
  2. Claim your cases. Click Claim & start onboarding on each case that's yours. Claiming:
    • makes you the pro's OS, if nobody is assigned yet;
    • moves the pro to Onboarding scheduled;
    • creates the 10 pro onboarding tasks;
    • opens the onboarding wizard.
  3. Work the wizard (below). You can stop at any point with Save & exit. It reopens at the same step from the pro's … menu › Onboarding wizard.
  4. Work My Tasks. Check off welcome emails, intake-call scheduling, organizers and 8821s as they happen, not at the end of the day.
  5. Look for flags. An orange OS needs manual assignment tag (Inbox) or Assign OS tag (pro header) means the Salesforce case owner didn't match anyone on the roster. Set the right person under Overview › Assignments › Onboarding specialist.

Use Dismiss only for cases that aren't a real onboarding: a duplicate, a test, or a case sent to the wrong team. The pro record stays; only the Inbox card goes away. If a pro turns out not to be ours, also archive it (pro … › Archive pro) with the reason.

05Tax Advisor

AdvisorStart in Home → My TasksReceives from OSHands off to Preparer

Daily loop

  1. Open Home. It shows tasks due today and overdue, your open projects, the at-risk count, and new Inbox cases.
  2. Clear My Tasks from the top. Work Overdue first, then Today, then look ahead at This week. Click a row to open its pro or project; click the circle to check it off.
  3. Review your book. Use the sidebar's Starred links: My pros, At-risk pros, My projects. On Projects, group by Stage to see what's stuck.
  4. Log every contact with the pro. Add a note on the pro or project (Ctrl/⌘ + Enter posts it). Then update Overview › Risk › Last advisor touchpoint.

How you get new pros

Tax Manager assigns new pros automatically. It picks the active advisor who is accepting new clients and has the fewest active pros; ties go to whoever was assigned least recently. The Reports page shows everyone's count and who's next.

To pause new assignments, during PTO or a heavy season, ask your manager to set you to not accepting new pros. Your existing book doesn't change.

Extensions

  1. Decide by day 7 of the project. On the project's Overview, set Extension needed? to Yes or No and check off Determine if extension is needed.
  2. If no: use … › Skip (not applicable) on File extension and Verify extension transmission acceptance.
  3. If yes: file it, set Extension filed? to Yes, and check off both tasks once the IRS accepts it. Reports then measure on-time against the extended deadline (+6 months).

At-risk pros

Mark a pro at risk when it might churn, miss a deadline or escalate. On the pro's Overview › Risk, set At risk to Yes and write a reason a manager can act on, for example “No docs after 3 follow-ups; deadline 3/15”. The pro then shows on the At-risk report and in the daily reports sheet. Setting it back to No clears the reason.

Handing a pro to another advisor

  1. Change Tax advisor on the pro's Overview. Its open pro tasks move to the new advisor.
  2. Change Tax advisor on each open project too. Projects keep their own advisor, so this second step is required.
  3. Leave a handoff note on the pro: status, open promises, anything sensitive.

Your part is done when

The extension decision is recorded on every project
The client review call is logged and the 8879 task is done
Every at-risk flag has a current reason, or has been cleared
The last touchpoint on each pro is within your team's contact window

06Tax Preparer

PreparerStart in My Tasks, Projects › BoardReceives from AdvisorHands off to Peer, then Advisor

Daily loop

  1. Open My Tasks and pick the return with the nearest due date. You'll see your prep, peer-review rework, e-file and acceptance tasks here. Peer Review in the sidebar shows every return you've sent to review.
  2. Click Start timer on the project before you start working it. The running timer shows in the top bar on every screen; click it to stop and log the time.
  3. Work the Tax Return Preparation tasks in order and check each one off as you finish it. Complete Income/Deduction Info has three subtasks, and it checks itself when they're all done.
  4. When you need something from the pro, add it under Outstanding items (below) and move the project to Waiting for pro.
  5. Stop the timer when you switch returns, take a break, or finish for the day.

The work timer

  • You can run one timer at a time. Starting a second one asks whether to Stop that & start here.
  • A timer left running stops on its own after 8 hours. The entry is marked auto-stopped and capped at 8 hours. Tell your manager the real time so it can be corrected.
  • See all logged time on the project's Time & stages tab, broken out by person. Managers see the totals on Reports.

Outstanding items

Outstanding items are the list of things the return is waiting on: a missing 1099, a mileage log, a prior-year return. Keep them on the project instead of in your inbox, so the advisor and BK lead can chase them too.

  1. Project › Outstanding items › Add item. Describe it the way you'd ask the pro, pick an owner (usually the advisor or BK lead who'll follow up) and a due date. Press Enter.
  2. Move the project to Waiting for pro if prep can't continue.
  3. Check the item off when it arrives (the circle marks it received). Resolved items are hidden; Show resolved brings them back.
  4. Once nothing is open, move the project back to Prep, or submit it for peer review if prep is finished.

Sending to peer review and back

Peer review now happens in Tax Manager instead of the Slack workflow. Everything the Slack form asked for is on the project's Peer review tab.

  1. Open the project's Peer review tab and click Submit for peer review (also in the project's … menu). Org ID, company name, return year and return type fill in from the project. Enter:
    • Estimated safe harbor tax payment amount.
    • Return add-ons. Pick every one that applies, or No add-ons. Only pick Additional K-1s for more than 4 partners. For Additional State(s), list the states in Comments.
    • Assigned tax advisor, filled in from the project.
    • Reviewer, filled in if the project already has a peer reviewer. Leave it blank to post the return to the queue for any reviewer to claim.
    • Comments: anything the reviewer needs to know, such as transcripts used or open questions.
    You're recorded as the person submitting. Submitting moves the return to QA, checks off Self-review and submit for peer review, gives the reviewer a task due in 2 days, and posts a note on the project.
  2. If the reviewer sends it back, you get an Apply peer-review changes task due in 2 days, and their notes show in an orange Needs rework box on the Peer review tab. Fix the return.
  3. Click Resubmit and say what you changed. The same reviewer gets it back as the next round, and your rework task checks itself off.
  4. When the reviewer marks it complete, the return usually moves to Client review on its own. Tell the advisor it's ready for the client.
Good to know
  • A return can have only one open review at a time.
  • You can't review a return you submitted.
  • If you submitted by mistake, use Cancel review and give a reason. You can submit again later.

Filing

  1. Once the signed 8879 is on file, move the project to Filing & delivery.
  2. E-file, then enter the E-file date on the project's Overview. The filing tasks are dated from it: E-file return on the day, Confirm IRS acceptance the day after, and Deliver signed copies to Drive folder two days after. Until you set it, those tasks have no due date.
  3. Check off E-file return and Confirm IRS acceptance once the IRS accepts it.
  4. After the BK lead delivers the signed copies, click Advance stage to Complete.

Peer review Peer reviewer

Claiming a review needs the Peer Reviewer role on the team roster. Everyone else can see the queue but not claim from it.

  1. Open Peer Review in the sidebar. The number next to it counts returns waiting for any reviewer, plus reviews that are waiting on you. The tabs are Waiting for reviewer, In review, Needs rework and Completed. Tick Only mine to narrow the list.
  2. Click Claim on a waiting return. This works like the Slack CLAIM button. You become the project's peer reviewer, and Peer Review Verification lands in your My Tasks. Returns submitted straight to you are already on your task list, but still need a claim.
  3. Open the return. The Peer review tab shows everything the preparer submitted and the 10-item checklist:
    • Taxpayer name + SSN verified
    • Spouse name + SSN verified
    • DOB(s) match prior year
    • Address matches IRS records
    • Fees recorded correctly
    • Engagement letter signed + on file
    • E-file diagnostics resolved
    • State filings included where required
    • Estimated payments accounted for
    • Return PDF previewed for legibility
    Tick each item as you verify it.
  4. If something needs fixing, click Needs rework and say exactly what to fix: form, line, and what's wrong. The preparer gets a task and your notes, and the notes also go on the project's Notes tab. When they resubmit, the return comes back to you as the next round.
  5. When it's right, click Review complete. This replaces the Slack REVIEW COMPLETE button. Add optional notes for the preparer. Leave Move the return to Client review ticked unless the advisor wants to hold it. Your task checks itself off.

If you can't finish a review you claimed, click Release to queue so another reviewer can take it.

Your part is done when

The e-file date is set and the IRS has accepted the return
Every outstanding item is resolved, or explained in a note
No timer is left running on the return
The project shows Complete

07Manager

Exec / AdminStart in ReportsAlso owns Settings, Templates, the team roster

Managers need the Exec or Admin role to see Settings and to edit templates. Without it, Templates is read-only.

Daily: 10-minute pipeline check

  1. Projects, grouped by Stage. Look at every Blocked project and its reason. Move each In queue (escalated) return forward, or reassign it.
  2. At-risk pros (Home tile or Starred link). Every one should have a current reason and a recent touchpoint.
  3. Reports › Active pros per advisor. Check the load is even and the right people are accepting new pros.
  4. Inbox. Claim or reassign any case that has sat unclaimed for more than a day.
  5. Peer Review. Nothing should sit in Waiting for reviewer for more than a day, and nothing should sit in Needs rework past its task due date.

Changing the standard task lists

  1. Open Templates and choose Pro templates or Project templates.
  2. Add, edit, reorder or switch off tasks. Each task has a section, a default role, a due anchor with an offset in days (negative means before the anchor), an optional checklist, and optional filing types it applies to.
  3. Click Preview materialization to see exactly what a new record would get.
  4. Click Publish version with a short note on what changed and why.
Good to know

Template edits apply only to pros claimed and projects created after the edit. Existing records keep their tasks. To change a return that's already in progress, add, skip or rename its tasks directly.

Archiving

Archive instead of deleting. Use the … menu on a pro or project and give a reason (churned, duplicate, created in error). Archived records leave every active list, report and advisor load count, and they keep their full history. Archive in the sidebar lists them, with Restore.

08How-to reference

Quick answers for the screens everyone uses.

Find a pro

Type an Org ID, business name or DBA in the search bar at the top. Use ↑ ↓ and Enter to open one; Esc clears the search.

Move through records

  • Records open in a side drawer over the list.
  • J / K go to the previous or next record.
  • Esc closes the drawer.
  • … › Copy link gives a link to share in Slack.

Edit a field

Click any value on the Overview tab. Enter or clicking away saves it; Esc cancels. A small check appears once it's saved.

Add a task or subtask

  • Add task: type a name, pick a section, assignee and due date, then press Enter. The box stays open for the next one.
  • For a subtask, use the task's … › Add subtask.

Change a task

  • Click the due date or assignee to change it.
  • Double-click the title to rename it.
  • Drag the handle to reorder within a section.
  • Hide done declutters the list.

Skip or delete

If a standard task doesn't apply, use … › Skip (not applicable). Skipped tasks don't count against progress, and the record shows it was considered. Delete only tasks you created by mistake.

Lists: filter, sort, group

Pros and Projects have preset views (All, My, At risk, Onboarding, 1040 + Sched C, Open, Pending peer review) plus Filter, Sort, Group and column controls. Save a combination as your own view and it appears under Starred in the sidebar.

Notes

Use the Notes tab on a pro or project for anything a teammate would need to pick the work up: calls, promises, decisions. Ctrl/⌘ + Enter posts. You can edit or delete only your own notes.

Create a pro by hand

Use Pros › New pro only when a case didn't come through Salesforce. The Org ID must be the real, numbers-only HCP Org ID, and a business name is required. An advisor is assigned automatically.

Add a return

On the pro, click Add project and pick the filing type and tax year. The deadline fills in (Mar 15 for S-Corp and partnership returns, Apr 15 for 1040s and C-Corps) and the 25 standard tasks are created.

Files

The pro's Drive folder button opens Business (Org ID)-TAX, or creates it if it doesn't exist. On a project, Files opens the same folder.

History

The Audit tab shows who changed what, and when, on a pro or project. Time & stages shows every stage move, the days spent in each stage, and exports to CSV.

09Ground rules

These keep the reports trustworthy and the handoffs clean.

  • If it isn't in Tax Manager, it didn't happen.No side spreadsheets, and no new work in Asana. Reports and handoffs only see what's here.
  • Move the stage when the work moves.Stage timing drives the cycle-time report and the Board. A late move makes the wrong person look slow.
  • Blocked always gets a specific reason.“Waiting on 2024 return from prior CPA”, not “waiting”. Unblock the same day the blocker clears.
  • Log every pro contact as a note.The next person to open the record needs it, and so do managers when a pro escalates.
  • Run the timer whenever you're working a return, and stop it when you stop.Logged hours feed the per-advisor summary and pricing decisions.
  • Skip standard tasks; don't delete them.Skipping shows the step was considered. Deleting hides that it ever existed.
  • Enter the e-file date before Complete.On-time reporting and the filing tasks' due dates depend on it.
  • Archive, never delete, pros and projects.Archiving keeps the audit trail and can be undone.
  • Don't edit the Tax Manager DB spreadsheet.The app keeps it consistent. Only managers edit the Identity tab; everything else goes through the app.
  • Keep client documents in the pro's Drive folder.Not in email or personal Drive. Everyone on the pro can reach it from the Drive folder or Files button.

A task has no due dateIts anchor date isn't set yet, usually the e-file date.Set the date on the project Overview, or give the task a date by hand.
Tasks show a role but no personNobody is set in that role on the pro or project.Set the person on the Overview tab. Unassigned open tasks for that role fill in.
“Set the parent Drive folder … in Settings first”The shared client folder isn't configured.Ask a manager to add it in Settings.
Timer entry marked auto-stoppedA timer ran past 8 hours and was capped.Tell your manager the real time.
Blank page, or stuck on “Loading Tax Manager…”The page didn't finish loading.Reload once. If it's still blank, tell your manager. They can add ?diag=1 to the link for a health check.

BGlossary

Org ID
The pro's Housecall Pro organization number. It's the one key for a pro in Tax Manager.
Pro
A Housecall Pro customer business we do tax work for.
Project
One tax return, or one-off filing such as an S-Corp election, for one pro and tax year.
TY
Tax year, e.g. TY2025 is filed in 2026.
OS
Onboarding Specialist.
BK / BK lead
Bookkeeping. The BK lead is the bookkeeper responsible for the pro's books and document collection.
IAC call
The intake call with the pro. Its answers are captured in wizard stage B.
INHA
The pro's bookkeeping context: whether HCP keeps the books, the prior books status, the payroll servicer and books access. Captured in wizard step A4.
Organizer
The document checklist the pro fills in and returns with their tax documents.
Form 8821
IRS Tax Information Authorization. Lets us view the pro's IRS records.
Form 8879
IRS e-file Signature Authorization. The pro signs it before we e-file.
WFP
Waiting for pro, the pipeline stage for returns paused on the pro.
QA
The peer review stage.
Round
One pass of a peer review. Each resubmission after rework starts the next round.
Safe harbor
The estimated tax payment that avoids an underpayment penalty for the coming year. It's entered on every peer review.
Anchor
The date a template task's due date is counted from: the claim date, project start, filing deadline or e-file date.
Materialize
Copying the template task list onto a new pro or project.
SFDC
Salesforce. New tax onboarding cases come from its “New Tax Onboarding Cases” report.
